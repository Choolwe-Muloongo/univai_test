# UnivAI Admissions Letters and Student Flow

## Purpose

This document defines the official UnivAI admissions process and the professional content structure for the two core admissions letters:

1. Provisional Offer Letter
2. Official Admission Letter

The goal is to keep admissions clear for applicants, administrators, and enrolled students. The Offer Letter and Admission Letter must not be treated as the same document. They belong to different stages of the admissions journey.

---

## Official Admissions Flow

The approved UnivAI admissions flow is:

```text
Student applies
↓
Application submitted
↓
Admin reviews
↓
Admin sends offer
↓
Offer Letter appears
↓
Student accepts offer
↓
Admission Letter appears
↓
Student completes enrollment
↓
Student gets full student dashboard
```

---

## Status Meaning

| Status | Meaning | Main Portal | Letter Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| `submitted` | Applicant has submitted the application | Admissions Portal | No letter yet |
| `under_review` | Admin is reviewing eligibility and documents | Admissions Portal | No letter yet |
| `needs_info` | Applicant must provide missing or corrected information | Admissions Portal | No letter yet |
| `offer_sent` | Admin has approved the applicant for an offer | Admissions Portal | Offer Letter visible |
| `approved` | Legacy/alternative offer state; should behave like `offer_sent` | Admissions Portal | Offer Letter visible |
| `admitted` | Applicant accepted the offer and has been officially admitted | Admissions Portal + Enrollment | Offer Letter and Admission Letter visible |
| `enrolled` / active enrollment | Student completed enrollment requirements | Student Portal | Letters remain available as admissions history |
| `rejected` | Application was declined | Admissions Portal | No admission letter |

---

## Letter 1: Provisional Offer Letter

### When it appears

The Provisional Offer Letter appears after the admin reviews the application, assigns an intake, and sends an offer.

```text
Admin reviews
↓
Admin sends offer
↓
Offer Letter appears
```

### Purpose

The Offer Letter tells the applicant that UnivAI is offering them a place in a programme, subject to conditions such as document verification, fee payment, and offer acceptance.

This letter is not the same as final admission. It is a formal invitation to accept a place.

### Required fields

The Offer Letter must include:

- UnivAI name and official admissions office identity
- Document title: `Provisional Offer Letter`
- Application reference number
- Date issued
- Applicant full name
- Programme name
- Qualification level, when available
- School / faculty
- Department, when available
- Delivery mode
- Assigned intake
- Offer conditions
- Offer acceptance instructions
- Admissions office signature
- Document verification/footer note

### Recommended tone

The tone should be warm, official, and conditional.

It should say, in effect:

> UnivAI is pleased to offer you a place in this programme, subject to acceptance of the offer and completion of all admissions and enrollment requirements.

### Professional content structure

```text
UNIVAI
Office of Admissions and Academic Registry

PROVISIONAL OFFER LETTER

Reference: [APPLICATION_REFERENCE]
Date Issued: [DATE]

Dear [APPLICANT_NAME],

Congratulations. After reviewing your application, UnivAI is pleased to offer you provisional admission into the following programme:

Programme: [PROGRAMME_NAME]
Qualification: [QUALIFICATION_LEVEL]
School / Faculty: [SCHOOL_NAME]
Department: [DEPARTMENT_NAME]
Delivery Mode: [DELIVERY_MODE]
Intake: [INTAKE]

This offer is subject to:
1. Verification of all submitted academic and identity documents.
2. Acceptance of the offer through the UnivAI Admissions Portal.
3. Payment of applicable admissions and enrollment fees.
4. Completion of the official enrollment process.
5. Compliance with UnivAI academic and student policies.

Please log in to the UnivAI Admissions Portal to accept this offer and continue to enrollment.

Sincerely,
UnivAI Admissions Office
Office of Admissions and Academic Registry

Document Verification:
This document was generated by UnivAI and should be verified through the official UnivAI Admissions Portal.
```

## Letter 2: Official Admission Letter

### When it appears

The Official Admission Letter appears after the applicant accepts the offer.

```text
Offer Letter appears
↓
Student accepts offer
↓
Admission Letter appears
```

### Purpose

The Admission Letter confirms that the applicant has accepted the offer and has been officially admitted into UnivAI for a specific programme and intake.

This letter is stronger than the Offer Letter. It should feel final, formal, and suitable for official use.

### Required fields

The Admission Letter must include:

- UnivAI name and official admissions office identity
- Document title: `Official Admission Letter`
- Application/admission reference number
- Date issued
- Applicant full name
- Programme name
- Qualification level, when available
- School / faculty
- Department, when available
- Delivery mode
- Assigned intake
- Admission confirmation statement
- Enrollment instructions
- Student portal instructions
- Admissions office / academic registry signature
- Document verification/footer note

### Recommended tone

The tone should be formal, official, and confirmatory.

It should say, in effect:

> You have been officially admitted to UnivAI for the selected programme and intake. Complete enrollment to activate your full student dashboard.

### Professional content structure

```text
UNIVAI
Office of Admissions and Academic Registry

OFFICIAL ADMISSION LETTER

Reference: [APPLICATION_REFERENCE]
Date Issued: [DATE]

Dear [APPLICANT_NAME],

Following your acceptance of the offer issued by UnivAI, we are pleased to confirm that you have been officially admitted into the following programme:

Programme: [PROGRAMME_NAME]
Qualification: [QUALIFICATION_LEVEL]
School / Faculty: [SCHOOL_NAME]
Department: [DEPARTMENT_NAME]
Delivery Mode: [DELIVERY_MODE]
Intake: [INTAKE]

Your admission is recorded subject to completion of the final enrollment requirements, including module selection, confirmation of delivery mode, payment of applicable fees, and compliance with UnivAI academic policies.

Please proceed to the UnivAI Enrollment Portal to complete your enrollment. Once enrollment is completed, your full Student Dashboard will be activated.

Sincerely,
UnivAI Admissions Office
Office of Admissions and Academic Registry

Document Verification:
This document was generated by UnivAI and should be verified through the official UnivAI Admissions Portal.
```

## Portal Placement

### Admissions Portal

The Admissions Portal must show:

- Application status
- Required document uploads
- Admission fee status
- Offer Letter, when status is `offer_sent`, `approved`, or `admitted`
- Admission Letter, when status is `admitted`
- Offer acceptance button, only before the offer has been accepted
- Enrollment button, after admission

### Student Portal

The Student Portal should show active learning features only after enrollment is complete. It may also show a read-only admissions history section that links back to the Admissions Portal.

---

## Admin Rules

Admins should follow this decision order:

1. Review the application.
2. Request missing information if needed.
3. Assign an intake.
4. Send the Offer Letter.
5. Wait for the student to accept the offer.
6. Let the system issue the Admission Letter.
7. Let the student complete enrollment.
8. Activate the full student dashboard.

Admins should not normally skip straight from `submitted` to `admitted`. Direct admission should only be used for exceptional manual corrections.

---

## Recommended Admin UI

The admin admissions page should be organized into these sections:

1. Applicant Details
2. Academic Eligibility
3. Document Review
4. Intake Assignment
5. Offer Decision
6. Letter History
7. Enrollment Status

The main admin button should be `Send Offer`, not `Mark Admitted`.

---

## Final Rule

Offer Letter means:

```text
We are offering you a place.
```

Admission Letter means:

```text
You accepted the place and are officially admitted.
```

Enrollment means:

```text
You are now being activated as a full student.
```
